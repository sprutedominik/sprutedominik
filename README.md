### Dominik Sprute, Zurich

I turn work that people do by hand across several systems into tools that handle most of the steps, while a person stays in control. My background is an M.Sc. in Psychology and Cognitive Neuroscience with a focus on data (FSU Jena), a semester of quantitative methods at Università Cattolica in Milan, and VR research in Karolinska Institutet's Digital Psychiatry Lab.

**What I work on now**

- At a Swiss fintech company: bots and agents for operations and customer service, data analysis, and the cloud infrastructure they run on.
- In health and psychology: on-premise IT and a practice management app with local AI for a psychotherapy practice (project mandate), so patient data stays in the building; and ongoing research data processing with Karolinska Institutet's Digital Psychiatry Lab for a VR exposure study.

**How the work has changed**

Less of my time goes into typing code. More goes into precise specs, deciding what a system may do alone, and checks that catch mistakes, human or machine. The assistants I build write drafts; a person sends them. Generated text is checked against the source data for amounts, dates and codes. Coding agents sit an exam with planted bugs first, and "all tests pass" counts only after I rerun the suite.

**Impact so far**

- 14 bots and agents designed, 10 of them in production, covering offers, documents, identity checks, contract lookups and reporting.
- In full live operation, the first AI agent answered about 53 % of incoming messages on its own, from customers, dealers and insurers, at around 120 new requests per working day.
- Renewal offers: from about 30 minutes to about 2 per offer (−93 %). The team runs this many times a week, so the saving adds up fast.
- Insurance and total-loss cases: from over 3 hours of active work across 11 systems to 10–15 minutes per case (−90 %), now driven step by step from a single command.
- A practice management app with about 265 API endpoints, in daily use by the whole team, bringing scheduling, records and billing into one system.

**Beyond work**

I'm drawn to behavioral data, how people actually act rather than how we expect them to, but my projects range well beyond that. I treat each one a little like a piece of craft: it should have a reason to exist and add a chapter to a longer story.
